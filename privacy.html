01Overview

Sauna Install LLC ("saunainstall," "we," "us") runs saunainstall.com, a marketplace that connects people who want a sauna installed ("homeowners") with independent installation contractors ("contractors"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

By using the site you agree to this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

02Information we collect

Information you give us.

  • Account details: name, email address, phone number and password.
  • Homeowner project details: your project description, sauna type and specifications, site notes, photos and your location (typically address, city and ZIP code).
  • Contractor business details: business name and address, company website, service areas, licensing and past work information we use to verify you, and the proposals and bids you send.
  • Messages and support requests you send us.

Payment information. Card and bank details are collected and processed by our payment provider, Stripe. They go directly to Stripe. We do not see or store your full card number or bank account number. We receive limited information such as the card brand, the last four digits, and whether a payment succeeded.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, we and our hosting provider receive basic technical data such as IP address, browser type, device, pages viewed and the date and time of your visit. We use this to keep the site running, secure and working well.

03How we use your information

  • To create and run your account and show you the site's features.
  • To connect homeowners and contractors, including sharing project details and proposals between them.
  • To process contractor memberships and, where offered, payouts.
  • To verify contractors and help prevent fraud and abuse.
  • To send service emails such as new proposals, receipts, account and security notices.
  • To answer questions, provide support and improve the site.
  • To meet legal obligations and enforce our terms.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for third-party advertising.

04Who we share it with

  • Other users. When a homeowner lists a project, contractors in the service area can see the project details. When a contractor sends a proposal, the homeowner sees the contractor's business information and proposal. Homeowners decide whether to contact or hire a contractor.
  • Service providers who work for us, such as Stripe (payments), our website host, and our email delivery provider. They may use your information only to provide their services to us.
  • Legal and safety. We may disclose information if required by law or legal process, or to protect the rights, safety and property of users, the public or saunainstall.
  • Business transfers. If we are involved in a merger, sale or similar transaction, information may be transferred as part of it.

05Cookies and similar technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies that are needed for the site to work, such as keeping you signed in and remembering your choices. If we add analytics, we will describe them here. You can control cookies in your browser settings, though some features may stop working if you block them.

06How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is active and as long as needed to provide the service, resolve disputes, keep records and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. You can ask us to delete your account at any time (see Your choices).

07Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and working with providers that handle payment data under industry security standards. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

08Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop sending you non-essential emails, by writing to info@saunainstall.com. We will respond within a reasonable time and may need to verify your identity first. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local privacy laws, and we will honor valid requests as those laws require. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in the email. We may still send you essential service messages.

09Children

The site is for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us information, contact us and we will delete it.

10Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above, and for significant changes we will give additional notice, such as by email or a notice on the site.
