Common questions

Cancel a contractor membership

Email info@saunainstall.com from the address on your account and we will cancel it. You keep access through the end of the period you have paid for, and you will not be charged again.

Billing and refunds

Questions about a charge, your receipt or a refund? Email us with your account email and the date of the charge. Contractor memberships are $24.99 a month after a free first month, and refund requests are reviewed case by case.

Help for homeowners

Posting a sauna project and receiving proposals is free. If you need help with your listing, a proposal, or a contractor, write to us and include your project ZIP code.

Help for contractors

Trouble with sign-up, service areas, bidding or payouts? Write to us with your business name and we will sort it out.

Quick answers

How much does it cost?

Homeowners pay nothing to list a project or receive proposals. Contractors pay $24.99 a month for unlimited bids, with no per-lead fees and no commission on jobs. The first month is free.

Do you charge per lead or take a cut of the job?

No. The flat membership is the only fee, and contractors keep the full amount of every job.

How do I update my card or contact details?

Email us from your account address and we will update it. A self-serve billing page is planned.

Where is my data and who sees it?

See our Privacy Policy. Payment details are handled by Stripe and are never stored by us.
